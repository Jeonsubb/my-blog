# South Korean 신입/주니어 Developer Hiring Market, 2025–2026 (Backend/Infra Emphasis)

Research date: 2026-10-18. All figures carry their publication date and source. "Historical" marks data from before 2025. "Anecdotal" marks community, blog, or education-vendor content. Items marked "(search snippet only)" come from search-result summaries; the page itself was not fetched, so check them before quoting.

## Q1. Volume of developer postings and 신입 hiring, 2022 → 2026, and the role of AI

### Takeaway
Every source points the same way. The entry-level share of Korean software-developer postings fell from 53.5% (2022) to 37.4% (2024), according to KLI. In platform data for H1 2025, postings that explicitly targeted 신입 were under 1% of developer postings, yet 신입 candidates sent the most applications (29.5%). In 2026, 네이버 skipped its developer 공채 for the first time in seven years, and 카카오's new hires fell about 35% over two years. Korean reporting and the Bank of Korea link this to AI automating junior-level routine coding, but they also cite the post-2022 downturn and a shift toward profitability. Causation is argued, not proven.

### Cited Findings
**Macro / statistical (Korea)**
- KLI (한국노동연구원) report "최근 소프트웨어 개발자 수 및 직무 변화" (published Dec 2025): the 신입 share of software-developer postings fell from **53.5% (2022) to 37.4% (2024)**, down 16.1 percentage points. Over the same period research/engineering roles fell 11.8pp and all other jobs fell 5.6pp. — [트윅/서울신문 계열, 2026-01-06](https://www.twig24.com/news/2026/01/06/20260106500238)
- Same KLI report: developers with **under 3 years' experience fell by 9,000 YoY in 2024**, while those with 3+ years rose by 42,000. Developers in their 30s grew from 183,000 (H2 2018) to 212,000 (H2 2024), up 15.8%. — [트윅, 2026-01-06](https://www.twig24.com/news/2026/01/06/20260106500238)
- The same article says over 80% of developers use generative AI at work but does not name a source for the figure. A Chung-Ang University AI professor (이재성) is quoted saying experienced staff using AI have made training juniors a lower priority. — [트윅, 2026-01-06](https://www.twig24.com/news/2026/01/06/20260106500238)

- 사람인·점핏 H1 2025 developer market report (about 100,000 postings and 2.6 million applications): by experience level, **5–10 years = 39.7% of postings; 2 years = 2.3%; 1 year = 1.4%; 신입 = 0.8%**. By applications, **신입 = 29.5% (the largest group)**, 1 year 10.9%, 2 years 10.4%. — [이투데이, 2025-08-27](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2500510)

(search snippet only; vendor blog) — [캔디드 블로그](https://teamcandid.kr/blog/job-market-analysis-value-creation)

**Bank of Korea (Aug 2026), the strongest official AI-linkage evidence**
- BOK report (오삼일, 오영식; released 2026-08-18): youth (15–29) jobs fell by **285,000 between June 2022 and June 2026**. Of that drop, **268,000 (94%)** came from industries with high AI exposure. Youth employment fell **31.4% in information services** and **16.6% in computer programming/SI/system management** over the same period. The report says AI is automating junior-type routine cognitive tasks first, including data search, drafting, basic analysis, translation, and **simple coding**. It warns that fewer entry-level hires narrows the path to becoming skilled workers. — [에너지경제신문, 2026-08-19](https://ekn.kr/web/view.php?key=20260819028084086)
- KDI research (한요셉; seminar in March 2024, historical) found that rising AI impact over 2017–2022 reduced youth employment or wages. — [경향신문, 2025-09-18](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202509180600001)
- US studies widely cited in Korean press (context, not Korean data): a Harvard paper (Hosseini & Lichtinger, 2025-08-31; 285,000 firms) found **entry-level hiring about 7.7% lower** at generative-AI-adopting firms six quarters after adoption, while senior hiring kept rising. A Stanford/ADP study (Brynjolfsson et al.) found employment of 22–25-year-olds in AI-exposed jobs, including software development, **down about 13%** relative to low-exposure jobs. — [경향신문, 2025-09-18](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202509180600001)

**Company-level (2025 → 2026)**
- Feb 2025 survey of 네카라쿠배당토: **only 네이버 planned a 2025 신입 developer 공채**. 카카오, 라인, 쿠팡, 우아한형제들, 당근, and 토스 all said they had "no separate 신입 IT developer hiring plan" and would use 상시채용 if needed. The article says 신입 hiring had dropped noticeably from 2–3 years earlier, and attributes this to the post-boom downturn, COVID-era salary inflation, and the shift from growth to profitability. — [바이라인네트워크 × OKKY, 2025-02-12](https://byline.network/2025/02/12-386/)
- **네이버** held annual developer 공채 from 2019 to 2025. In 2021 it ran two rounds and hired more than 900, its peak. From 2023 it ran a joint "Team Naver" 공채 across 6 entities. **In H1 2026 it held no 신입 developer 공채**, the first gap in seven years, and as of mid-August 2026 had no H2 plan. A spokesperson said AI has changed work and needed skills, so Naver is re-examining its talent profile and evaluation criteria, and is filling needs through 경력채용. Naver headcount: 4,383 (2023) → 4,583 (2024) → 5,047 (2025). — [한국금융신문, 2026-08-14](https://www.fntimes.com/html/view.php?ud=202608141444496947094b7542a8_18); [에너지경제신문, 2026-08-19](https://ekn.kr/web/view.php?key=20260819028084086)
- **카카오** new hires: **452 (2023) → 314 (2024) → 292 (2025)**, down 35.4%. Under-30 new hires: 294 → 208 → 187, down 36.4%. Source is the Kakao 2025 Sustainability Report. — [에너지경제신문, 2026-08-19](https://ekn.kr/web/view.php?key=20260819028084086)
- 카카오's internal-hire ratio for open positions rose from **2.8% (2024) to 37% (2025)**. Employees under 30 fell from 989 (2023) to 615 (2025), while those aged 30–49 rose from 2,828 to 3,209. Analysts quoted say internal redeployment and efficiency outweigh the stated "AI talent" goal. — [딜사이트, 2026-07-22](https://dealsite.co.kr/articles/165674)
- Krafton was reported cutting staff despite record results as part of an "AI first" transition. (headline only, not fetched) — [뉴시스, 2025-11-19](https://mobile.newsis.com/view/NISX20251119_0003408895)

### Inferences
- The junior squeeze is structural, not a single-year dip. It starts with the 2022 peak-to-trough correction, continues through 2023–2024 cost-cutting, and in 2025–2026 is reinforced by AI-driven re-scoping of junior tasks. The BOK figures (computer programming/SI youth employment −16.6%, info services −31.4%, 2022–2026) are the most authoritative Korea-specific link to AI, but they cover industry-level youth employment, not 신입 developers specifically.
- Supply and demand are badly mismatched. 신입 are the largest applicant group (29.5%) for a tiny share of explicitly-신입 postings (0.8%). That gap is what produces the "중고신입" phenomenon (people with 1–2 years' experience applying to 신입 roles) and the push toward "경력 1–3년 포함" postings.
- Backend is both the most-posted and the most-applied function, so competition is highest there. DevOps/system engineering has a similar posting share (11.1%) but fewer applicants per posting.
- Volume is not uniformly collapsing. 원티드's survey shows 개발 is still the most-hired function, and AI-keyword 신입 postings are rising. Demand is shifting toward experienced hires and AI-adjacent skills more than disappearing.

### Gaps
- No primary 원티드 or 잡코리아 time series of 신입 developer postings for 2022–2026 was found. 원티드 publishes a "2025 개발자 리포트" ([wanted.co.kr/events/2025devreport](https://www.wanted.co.kr/events/2025devreport)), but its contents were not retrieved.
- No quantitative data specifically on "중고신입" developers (share of 신입 hires with prior experience) was found.
- No Korean survey directly asking employers "did AI coding tools reduce your junior hiring?" was found. Korean evidence is either macro (BOK, KLI) or company statements.
- 잡플래닛's 2026 H2 developer-hiring data articles (e.g., news-8357, news-8452) returned HTTP 403 and could not be read. A snippet says network/security/operations roles have the most 신입 postings, while backend has a high overall share but a low 신입 share. Unverified.

## Q2. Major companies' 신입 개발자 공채/인턴십 in 2025–2026 and selection processes

### Takeaway
The traditional big-group 공채 (삼성, LG CNS, 현대오토에버, banks) continued in 2026 with structured multi-stage processes. Platform companies (네카라쿠배당토) mostly moved to 수시 hiring, small-batch programs (토스 NEXT), or 전환형 인턴. 카카오's one-off group 공채 in Sept 2025 and 네이버's 2026 pause mark the shift. The common pipeline is 서류 → 코딩테스트 and/or 과제 → 1차 기술면접 → 2차/컬처핏 → (레퍼런스 체크) → 건강검진.

### Inferences
- For a 2026–2027 backend/infra 신입, the reliably open front doors are the conglomerate and SI 공채 (삼성 SW, LG CNS DX/Cloud, 현대오토에버 quarterly rounds), bank ICT tracks, and 전환형 인턴 programs. Platform companies are mostly reachable through 수시 postings that often require 0–3 years, small cohort programs (토스 NEXT), or affiliated bootcamps (우테코).
- 현대오토에버 and LG CNS explicitly list cloud/infra roles (Private Cloud, network/middleware/server engineer, Cloud Application Modernization) as 신입 entry points. That makes the SI/captive-IT tier the most accessible infra entry route.
- Conversion internships are spreading (크래프톤, 넥슨, 당근 ML, 토스뱅크). In practice the "interview" now often includes months of on-the-job evaluation.

### Gaps
- No 2026 신입 developer process was found for 쿠팡, 라인, 우아한형제들 (outside 우테코), 당근 backend, 삼성SDS (as a separate company), SK C&C/SK AX, 신한·우리·하나 IT, or 공기업 전산직 (e.g., NCS-based 전산직 at 한전KDN, 국민연금, etc.). Search coverage was limited.
- Official hire counts are missing for most programs. Only 토스 NEXT gives one ("up to 50").

## Q3. What coding tests look like now and whether AI is changing them

### Takeaway
Most large Korean employers (삼성, 카카오, 네이버, 현대오토에버, 토스) still use classic timed algorithm tests, and per 잡코리아 most still prohibit AI. Since 2025–2026 a visible minority has switched to "AI-allowed" or "AI-evaluated" formats that score how candidates prompt, verify, and debug AI output: 컬리, 무신사, 크래프톤, 넥슨, NHN, and reportedly LG CNS. Practical assignments (과제) on real-service-like codebases are gaining weight.

### Cited Findings
- **Samsung SW 역량테스트 (A형 level, used in 공채)**: 2 problems in about **240 minutes**, coded on a PC, with C/C++/Java/Python allowed. Per candidate reports, problem 1 tends to be hard implementation/simulation and problem 2 tends to be efficiency. Cutoffs are not disclosed. Anecdotal (community compilation). — [링커리어 커뮤니티](https://community.linkareer.com/employment_data/6625027). It replaces GSAT for the SW track in the 2026 H2 공채. — [데일리포스트, 2026-09-07](https://www.thedailypost.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=115442)
- **카카오 2026 그룹 공채**: two coding-test rounds, with Kakao publishing official 1차/2차 solution write-ups. — [tech.kakao.com/posts/814](https://tech.kakao.com/posts/814). Problem count, time limit, and AI policy could not be extracted from the page.
- **토스 NEXT 2025**: one-day online coding test **plus** a practical development task "based on real service operations". — [토스피드, 2025-07-30](https://toss.im/tossfeed/article/2025next)
- **현대오토에버**: role-dependent choice of language (Java/JS/Python/Go for backend; some roles allow C/C++/C#/Kotlin/Ruby/Swift). It splits 코딩테스트 roles from 과제테스트 roles. — [현대오토에버, 2026](https://career.hyundai-autoever.com/ko/2026newrecruit3q)
- **AI-allowed tests in Korea (잡코리아, 2026-05-09)**: **컬리 allows a generative-AI chatbot** within the test and evaluates the AI-use process and problem-solving approach. **무신사 evaluates Codex-use ability** alongside a regular coding test. **"네이버, 카카오 등 대부분" still prohibit AI.** The format is shifting from fixed algorithm problems toward reading an existing codebase, fixing bugs, adding features, and verifying results. 잡코리아 names four criteria: **문제 구조화, AI 제어력, 검증 습관, 커뮤니케이션**. Algorithm knowledge is still needed to judge the complexity and edge cases of AI-generated code. — [잡코리아 인사이트, 2026-05-09](https://www.jobkorea.co.kr/recruit/careers/articles/ai-enabled-coding-interview)
- 무신사's posting explicitly evaluates "Claude·Cursor 같은 AI Agent 활용 능력" separately. (search snippet of a vendor blog) — [코드트리 블로그](https://www.codetree.ai/blog/ko/2026-%EC%A3%BC%EB%AA%A9%ED%95%A0-%EB%A7%8C%ED%95%9C-%EA%B0%9C%EB%B0%9C%EC%9E%90-%EC%B1%84%EC%9A%A9-%EB%AA%A8%EC%9D%8C-%EB%AC%B4%EC%8B%A0%EC%82%AC-%EB%91%90%EB%82%98%EB%AC%B4-%EC%95%84)
- **Game industry**: 넥슨 replaced the coding test with an AI-use assessment, and 크래프톤 grades AI chat logs and verification steps (Cofa-Probe). NC keeps the coding test but adds AI-literacy checks. — [더팩트, 2026-10-15](https://news.tf.co.kr/read/economy/2376557.htm)
- **동아일보 (2026-09-12)**, "프롬프트까지 제출하세요": AI use is tolerated or encouraged in some take-home stages but generally **not allowed in live coding interviews**, where evaluators check that candidates can solve problems themselves. Recruiters focus more on problem definition and judging AI output than on raw speed, and spec-writing and "builder" skills are rising. The article cites a CoderPad survey of 650+ developers and hiring managers: about 34% treat AI use in tests as cheating or disallowed, about 29% do not, about 17% are unsure, and about 20% say it varies by test. About 66% value **finding errors in AI-generated output**. The page text was partly garbled, so verify against the original. — [동아일보 via 네이트, 2026-09-12](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260912n05091)
- **KB국민은행** IT/AI·플랫폼 tracks: no coding test in the 2026 H2 process. (search snippet) — [SNU CSE notice](https://cse.snu.ac.kr/en/community/notice/25516)

### Inferences
- A 2026 backend 신입 still has to be ready for classic 프로그래머스/백준-style algorithm tests (삼성 A형-level implementation/simulation, 카카오 multi-round). Most mainstream employers have not dropped them.
- The new skill to add is AI-assisted work under observation: reading unfamiliar code, using an AI agent, and showing verification (tests, edge cases, explaining why the AI was wrong). Live interviews will probe whether candidates understand the code without AI.
- Because 크래프톤 and similar firms grade AI chat logs, the candidate's prompting and verification trail is now part of the work sample.

### Gaps
- No official 2025–2026 data was found on 소프티어 (Hyundai Motor Group's coding platform) test format or certification levels, or on 삼성 B형/Pro certification changes.
- No platform statistics were found from 프로그래머스 or 백준 (e.g., number of companies using AI-allowed tests).
- Whether 카카오/네이버/토스 changed AI policy for their 2025–2026 tests was not confirmed from primary sources. Only 잡코리아's general statement that "most prohibit" exists.

## Q4. What hiring managers look for in 신입 backend candidates (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
Hard data shows employers want people who "contribute immediately". That favors candidates with real project or ops experience and the ability to verify AI output, over algorithm skill alone. Survey data ranks job-specific competence first, collaboration second, and AI/data use fourth. Portfolio advice (troubleshooting narratives with numbers, self-designed rather than cloned projects, GitHub/Notion links) is consistent across sources, but it comes mostly from education vendors and is anecdotal.

### Cited Findings
- OKKY survey of 71 hiring decision-makers (Feb 2025): 54% had **no** 신입 hiring plan, 40% planned to hire, and 7% were undecided. Among hirers, 58% expected only 1–2 신입. **Backend was the most-sought 신입 role (38%)**, then frontend (26.7%), data analysis (10%), and **infra (7%)**. Reasons for reluctance: economic downturn (about 36%) and **"need people who can contribute immediately" (35%)**. — [바이라인네트워크 × OKKY, 2025-02-12](https://byline.network/2025/02/12-386/)
- 원티드랩 2026 survey (153 HR managers; multiple responses allowed): **직무 전문 역량 64.7%**, 팀워크·협업 37.9%, 조직 기여 의지 28.1%, **AI·데이터 활용 역량 24.2%**. A 원티드랩 official: "AI를 활용해 업무 생산성을 높일 수 있는지가 인재 판단의 핵심 기준으로 떠오르고 있다." — [지디넷코리아 via Daum, 2025-12-08](https://v.daum.net/v/20251208161412362)
- 네이버 spokesperson (Aug 2026): solving problems with AI matters, but so does **the ability to critically review and judge AI output**. Industry sources say developers are expected to define problems and verify AI output, and **domain expertise** (AI search, ads, commerce, **cloud, security**) is rising in importance. — [한국금융신문, 2026-08-14](https://www.fntimes.com/html/view.php?ud=202608141444496947094b7542a8_18)
- 카카오 framed its 2025 group 공채 around "AI 네이티브" talent. CA협의체 chair 정신아 said today's young people are the first generation to grow up with diverse AI tools. — [에너지경제신문, 2026-08-19](https://ekn.kr/web/view.php?key=20260819028084086)
- 잡코리아 (2026-05): evaluation has moved toward **verifying and explaining AI output** over fast implementation, scored on 문제 구조화, AI 제어력, 검증 습관, and 커뮤니케이션. — [잡코리아 인사이트, 2026-05-09](https://www.jobkorea.co.kr/recruit/careers/articles/ai-enabled-coding-interview)
- At a 2026 H2 recruiting session, a company said AI is becoming a tool to test candidates' thinking, while **understanding, explaining, and taking responsibility for results** stays with the candidate. (search snippet) — [브랜드뉴스](https://www.ibrandnews.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=12927)
- Portfolio guidance (anecdotal, education vendors): recruiters look at how candidates solved technical problems hit during design more than at ERDs or architecture diagrams. Describe error and performance analysis **with concrete numbers**. Clone-coding presented as one's own work gets exposed in interviews, so self-planned or extended projects score better. GitHub/Notion links are effectively mandatory as of 2025. One CTO is cited saying communication-less 신입 won't be hired. (search snippets) — [제로베이스](https://zero-base.co.kr/event/media_insight_contents_BE_project); [prime-career](https://prime-career.com/article/16514); [코딩취업아카데미](https://keduitcenter.co.kr/%ED%8F%AC%ED%8A%B8%ED%8F%B4%EB%A6%AC%EC%98%A4-%ED%94%84%EB%A1%9C%EC%A0%9D%ED%8A%B8-%ED%9A%A8%EA%B3%BC%EC%A0%81%EC%9D%B8-%EA%B5%AC%EC%84%B1%EB%B2%95%EC%9D%80/)
- 토스's 2025 process includes a **컬처핏 interview and reference check** even for ≤3-year candidates, and its 과제 is based on real service operations. — [토스피드, 2025-07-30](https://toss.im/tossfeed/article/2025next)

### Inferences
- "Can contribute immediately" plus "verifies AI output" points to portfolio evidence of operating something real: deployed services, measured performance improvements, incident or troubleshooting write-ups, tests, and CI. For infra-leaning candidates, that means hands-on cloud/IaC/observability work, which matches the rising emphasis on cloud and security domain knowledge.
- Infra was the least-sought 신입 role in the OKKY survey (7%). Infra entry is therefore more likely through SI/captive IT (현대오토에버, LG CNS) or via backend roles that include DevOps duties than through pure "신입 SRE" postings.

### Gaps
- No Korean employer survey specifically quantifying the value of tech blogs, open-source contributions, test coverage, or performance-improvement metrics for 신입 backend hiring was found. Those claims rest on vendor and community advice.
- No primary hiring-manager interviews from 토스/당근/우아한형제들 engineering blogs about 신입 backend criteria in 2025–2026 were retrieved.

## Q5. Role of bootcamps and talent programs (우테코, SSAFY, 부스트캠프, 카카오테크 부트캠프, SW마에스트로, 42서울)

### Takeaway
Corporate-run programs are now a main 신입 pipeline as open 공채 shrink. SSAFY reports about 85% cumulative employment and preferential screening at 185 firms, and 우아한테크코스 is effectively 우아한형제들's 신입 channel. The sector is volatile: Naver halted 부스트캠프 for 2026. Of the programs reviewed, only SSAFY publishes an employment rate, and it is self-reported by Samsung.

### Cited Findings
- **SSAFY (삼성청년SW·AI아카데미)**: about 11,000 graduates through cohort 13, of whom **9,396 are employed (about 85%)**. Graduates work at **2,600+ companies**, and **185 preferred employers** offer document-screening exemptions or bonus points. **840+ graduates** have joined the five major banks under a 2023 agreement (banks donated ₩7.5B over 3 years). Named employers include 삼성전자, 현대오토에버, 롯데이노베이트, and BNK부산은행. Curriculum is 1,725 hours, of which **about 1,025 hours (about 60%) are AI**. Students get free tuition and ₩1M/month. — [더나은미래(조선일보), 2026-06-30](https://futurechosun.com/archives/153207)
  - Reported rates vary by date: Dec 2025 reports gave **10,125 graduates and 85%** (search snippet — [네이트](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20251218n33314)). Jun 2025 reported 80% for cohorts 1–11 and early cohorts 68% (search snippets — [서울경제](https://www.sedaily.com/article/13096211)). The definition of "employed" is not disclosed.
- **네이버 부스트캠프**: Naver Connect Foundation **halted new operations of 부스트캠프 web·mobile (since 2016) and AI Tech (since 2021) for 2026**. These had produced several hundred developers per year. Naver says it is being restructured and will return in a new format. — [에너지경제신문, 2026-08-19](https://ekn.kr/web/view.php?key=20260819028084086)
- **우아한테크코스 8기**: recruited in Sept–Oct 2025 (documents 9/29–10/10) for **web backend**, web frontend, and mobile Android. Selection used a 5-week 프리코스. About 10 months of training starts Feb 2026. (search snippet of an SNU CSE notice) — [SNU CSE notice](https://cse.snu.ac.kr/community/notice/24259). No employment-rate data was found.
- **카카오테크 부트캠프**: cohort 2 graduated 2025-08-01 (131 graduates). Cumulative output is **550**, with a target of 1,000 by 2026. Cohort 2 tracks were AI 실무 개발, 풀스택, and **클라우드 네이티브**. Cohort 4 recruits 150 and includes resume and portfolio career coaching. It runs under the MOEL K-Digital Training program. (search snippets of Kakao press releases) — [카카오 2기 수료](https://www.kakaocorp.com/page/detail/11648); [카카오 4기 모집](https://www.kakaocorp.com/page/detail/11963). No employment rate is published.
- **SW마에스트로 (과기정통부/IITP)**: the 16th cohort (Apr 2025) was selected at a **19:1** competition rate. The 15th had 3,000+ applicants (15:1). Through cohort 14, it produced 140+ graduate-founded startups. (search snippets) — [디지털데일리, 2025-04-03](https://m.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2025040315074033683). No employment rate is published.
- **42서울**: no 2025–2026 employment data found.
- Context: Nexon, NHN, and Krafton increasingly run hackathon or internship-based selection (NHN's NAN 2026 hackathon led to final interviews). — [더팩트, 2026-10-15](https://news.tf.co.kr/read/economy/2376557.htm)

### Inferences
- With 공채 shrinking at platforms, corporate-affiliated programs work as extended interviews. They give a credible signal (selective admission, long project periods, company-network screening perks) that partly substitutes for experience. SSAFY's 185 preferred employers and bank ties make it the most measurable pipeline to finance IT and SI.
- Program availability is itself unstable (부스트캠프 pause), and curricula are pivoting hard to AI (SSAFY about 60% AI hours). Backend/infra candidates should expect AI integration to be part of the program portfolio.

### Gaps
- No published employment rates were found for 우아한테크코스, 카카오테크 부트캠프, SW마에스트로, or 42서울.
- No independent (non-sponsor) verification of SSAFY's 85% figure, nor of its employer-tier breakdown (share of large firms or of backend roles), was found.
